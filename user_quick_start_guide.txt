Waste Collection Display – Quick Start

1. Power it up
- Plug the power cable into the micro-USB power port
It will turn on automatically.

2. Connect it to Wi-Fi
Everything is already installed and configured for your address.
- It just needs internet:
- After a minute, the e-ink screen will show: SSID: waste-collection-setup
- On your phone or laptop, connect to the Wi-Fi network called waste-collection-setup
- A setup page will appear — select your home Wi-Fi network and enter the password
- The device will reboot itself and start working

3. After that
From now on:
- You only need to give it power
- It checks for new collection dates several times a day
- It will quietly keep itself updated
